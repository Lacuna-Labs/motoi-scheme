## The appendix

**Title:** *Jesse's Making Beats*

**Placement:** appendix in Book of Jesse (Motoi variant).

**Content shape:** how to start making a sound → how to move up to making
beats inside Motoi. From a single tone to a rhythm to a full chiptune loop.
Kid-friendly. Runnable in the fantasy console mode. Should end with the
kid making an actual beat and saving it as a cart.

## The origin story (context, not literal chapter text)

Two decades ago, Jesse was at work. They were supposed to be doing work.
Something happened — Alfred doesn't remember what — but Jesse started
making chiptune beats instead. It was really hilarious. It became a thing:
"Jesse's making beats." That is what this appendix is named after.

The appendix is a small tribute inside the ramp-up book. Kids learning
Sakura/Motoi Scheme meet Jesse (the character in the onboarding book) and
by the appendix, they get to make beats too — because that's what Jesse
would do.

## Voice + shape

- First-person Sakura teaching voice, gentle.
- Real runnable Scheme using Motoi's sound / framebuffer / animation
  primitives.
- Not a music-theory lecture — a "here is the fun; here is how to press
  the buttons; go make one" walk-through.
- Sub-sections: your first tone, your first note, your first two notes,
  your first bar, your first beat, your first loop, save-and-share.
- Reference the fantasy-console mode chapter earlier in the book.
- Reference Book of Instruments (which lives in scheme-books/) for depth.
- Reference Book of Music (poetry + form) for the *why* if the kid is curious.
