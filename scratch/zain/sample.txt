  :summary "caar: take the first item, take the first item. Reads as car then car, and answers an ELEMENT."
  :explanation "One of the twenty-eight composed accessors of R7RS-small 6.4.1. Decode the name by dropping the leading c and the trailing r, then reading the 2 letters RIGHT TO LEFT: aa gives car then car -- take the first item, take the first item. Written out it is (car (car pair)). The leading letter a tells you the answer is an ELEMENT: a name whose leftmost letter is a answers an element, a name whose leftmost letter is d answers the rest of a list. You meet it on an alist of settings, where the job is reading the KEY of the first entry of an alist. On a mis-shaped input caar answers a value that is neither a pair nor '() and yet is TRUE in an if -- a non-value. R7RS-small 6.4.1 defines it as (car (car pair)), and that expansion RAISES. That is a divergence from the standard and it is why this record is flagged: a walk that over-reaches will not stop, it will hand you something you cannot print and that passes an if. Two neighbouring jobs get mistaken for this one. For a depth that is not fixed, list-ref (for an element) or list-tail (for a tail) takes the number instead of spelling it in letters. For a path that mixes both, write the 2 steps out as car and cdr -- they raise where the standard says to."
