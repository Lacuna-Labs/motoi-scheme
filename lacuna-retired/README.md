# Lacuna, retired parts (2026-11-15)

These are parts of Lacuna that the 1.0 build no longer runs: the JavaScript foreman (`lacuna-foreman/`, replaced by the
foreman in the Lacuna VM), the Python broker, job runner, cortex and helpers that ran from the sandbox (`jail/`), and the
Python tools gathered in `python/`. They are kept here, in Motoi, as a record and for the people who may learn from them.
They are not maintained and are not what Lacuna runs.
