# Lacuna, retired parts (2026-11-15)

These are parts of Lacuna that the 1.0 build no longer runs: the JavaScript foreman (`lacuna-foreman/`, replaced by the
foreman in the Lacuna VM), the Python broker, job runner, cortex and helpers that ran from the sandbox (`jail/`), and the
Python launchers and tools gathered in `python/` (including `bin/lacuna-py`, `bin/lacuna-mcp`, `tools/arcade/*.py`,
`layers-solve.py`, `stack-aliases.py`, and the Slat/Scheme token probes). They are kept here, in Motoi, as a record and
for the people who may learn from them. They are not maintained and are not what Lacuna runs.
